METHOD
Command: CMAKE_GENERATOR=Ninja bash repro-deflate.sh
All default levels (1-12) and all 12 Silesia files were used.
Levels 1-9: three rounds, five timed repetitions per engine per round (15 samples per engine/file/level).
Levels 10-12: two rounds, three timed repetitions per engine per round (6 samples per engine/file/level).
For each level/file/round, libdeflate ran first, then roc-deflate. Every timed repetition was retained.
Both engines used the same mimalloc shared library via LD_PRELOAD.
C: cc -O3; Roc compiler: ReleaseFast; Roc harness: roc build --opt=speed.
Corpus reads, initial compression, verification, and output writes are outside the timed interval.
C reuses one compressor and a caller-allocated output buffer across repetitions.
Roc calls Deflate.compress for each repetition; the measured API includes its allocation/cleanup behavior.
The timing is compression-only; it is not a decompression benchmark.
